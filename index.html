clip-bot

clip-bot is a personal tool that prepares short highlight clips from Twitch streamers who permit clipping and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube channel as Shorts.

Every video credits the original streamer by name and links back to their Twitch channel and the original clip.

clip-bot only uploads to the single YouTube channel owned by its operator. It does not access, collect or post on behalf of any other users.
