Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

What clip-bot is

clip-bot is a personal tool operated by a single person. It uploads short video clips to the operator's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

What data it accesses

clip-bot requests only the youtube.upload permission, which lets it upload videos to the channel of the account that signs in. It does not read, collect or store any other Google account data, and it does not access data belonging to any other users.

How data is stored

The sign-in token that lets clip-bot upload is stored only on the operator's own computer. It is never sold, shared or sent to third parties.

YouTube and Google

clip-bot uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on clip-bot's Google sign-in screen.
